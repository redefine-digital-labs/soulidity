SEGA
The Organization

DeFi products and infrastructure built around the attention economy. Attention has always existed — SEGA builds the systems that make it flow and compound.

Soul
The Carrier

Every era of the attention economy has its primary carrier — coins, tokens, NFTs. Soul is the carrier for this era. Dynamic, identity-bound, perpetually accumulating.

Soulidity
The Infrastructure

The protocol for minting, holding, authorizing, and trading Souls. SEGA's current core product, built on Sui. Soul is the asset. Soulidity is the rails.
