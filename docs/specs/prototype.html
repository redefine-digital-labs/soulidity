How it works

Migrate · Expand
Expand to Soul
Wrap an existing NFT with a Soul layer — the original NFT stays unchanged. You add identity, memory, and skills on top, making it a Soul without changing ownership.
🔗 Wrap+Link model — Your NFT contract and token IDs are untouched. Soulidity registers a Soul layer on-chain that points to each NFT. Holders retain all original rights plus gain Soul-layer capabilities.
👤
Personal Join
You hold an NFT and want to add your own Soul layer. No collection-wide agreement needed — just your NFT, your Soul.
🌐
Collection Join
You are the creator of an NFT collection and want to expand the entire collection. Sets a Soul template that all holders can activate.
⚡ Conflict policy — If a holder completes Personal Join first and a creator later runs Collection Join on the same NFT, the holder's personal data is always canonical. The collection template becomes an optional notification the holder can choose to activate or ignore.
